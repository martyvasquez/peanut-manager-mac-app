# Peanut Manager Support

Questions, problems or ideas: email CONTACT_EMAIL, or open an issue at https://github.com/martyvasquez/peanut-manager-mac-app/issues.

## Common questions

**Do I need AI?** No. Drag players into the batting order, pick positions on the field (a taken spot swaps, so two players never share one), and print. AI is an extra button on the same screens.

**How do I turn on AI?** Settings → sign in with your ChatGPT account (Go, Plus or Pro), or paste a Claude API key from https://console.anthropic.com. You use one at a time.

**Where do stats come from?** Export your season stats from GameChanger as a CSV and import it on the Stats & Insights page. Peanut Manager isn't affiliated with GameChanger.

**Are my rules always followed?** Rules with a check are verified on every lineup, and the lineup shows whether it follows them. Other rules are weighed by the AI and marked that way. Spots you lock are always kept.

**Where is my data?** On your devices and in your private iCloud account. See the [privacy policy](privacy).
