# Peanut Manager Privacy Policy

*Effective October 23, 2026*

Peanut Manager helps youth baseball and softball coaches make lineups. It's built so your team's information stays yours.

## The short version

- Your teams, players, stats and lineups are stored on your devices and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud account.
- We don't run servers, we don't have accounts, and we never see your data.
- No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
- AI is optional. If you use it, your team's information goes directly from your device to the AI company you chose (OpenAI or Anthropic), only after you allow it.

## What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter or import (teams, players, positions, ratings, notes, season stats, rules, games and lineups) is stored by the app on your device.

**iCloud.** If you're signed in to iCloud, the app keeps your data in sync across your devices using your private iCloud account. Everything about your players (names, jersey numbers, notes, ratings, positions, stats, scouting reports, attendance and lineups) and your rules are end-to-end encrypted, so Apple can't read them. Team names, opponent names and game dates use iCloud's standard encryption. We have no access to your iCloud data.

**Keychain.** Your ChatGPT sign-in and your Claude API key are kept in your device's keychain. Your Claude API key can sync through iCloud Keychain so you only enter it once.

**Imports.** When you import a stats file (for example, a GameChanger CSV export), the app reads only the file you choose, on your device.

## AI features (optional)

The app works fully without AI. If you choose to use AI to suggest lineups or write scouting reports, you connect one AI service:

- **ChatGPT**, by signing in with your own ChatGPT account, or
- **Claude**, with your own Anthropic API key.

The first time you use AI with a service, the app asks your permission and names the company. Only after you allow it, and only when you use an AI feature, the app sends that company what it needs to do the job: your players' names, stats, ratings, positions and notes, who's coming to the game, your team rules, your notes for the game, the opponent scouting report, and the team's age group.

This information goes directly from your device to OpenAI or Anthropic under your own account. It never passes through us. How they handle it is covered by their policies:

- OpenAI: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
- Anthropic: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy

You can stop using AI at any time by signing out of ChatGPT or removing your Claude key in Settings.

## What we collect

Nothing. We don't operate servers, collect analytics or crash reports, show ads, or track you. If you share crash reports with Apple, Apple may share them with us as developers; they don't include your team's data.

## Children

Peanut Manager is for coaches, not players. Coaches enter information about the players on their team, and that information stays in the coach's control as described above. We don't collect information from children. Before using AI features, coaches should be comfortable sharing their players' information with the AI company they choose.

## Deleting your data

Delete a player, a team or the app to remove the data from your device. If you use iCloud, deleting data in the app removes it from your iCloud account too, and you can manage it under System Settings → [your name] → iCloud. Data you sent to OpenAI or Anthropic is governed by their policies and your account settings with them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
